ME21N is used to create a Purchase Order in SAP Materials Management (MM).
ME23N is used to display an existing Purchase Order.
ME22N is used to change a Purchase Order.

FB60 is used to enter a vendor invoice in SAP Financial Accounting (FI).
FB03 is used to display accounting documents.

To raise an SAP access request:
1. Identify the required role.
2. Check for SoD conflicts.
3. Submit the request via SAP GRC or access management system.
4. Wait for manager and security approvals.

Segregation of Duties (SoD) ensures that critical tasks are divided among different users to prevent fraud.
